Research

My research combines explainable AI, predictive modelling, and decision-support systems to address challenges in healthcare, operations, and financial crime detection. As a Research Assistant at UEL, I work on applied healthcare AI, with wider interests in NLP and computer vision. My upcoming PhD focuses on AI-driven fraud detection.
