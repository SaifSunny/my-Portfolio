What I Do

Applied AI & Machine Learning

I build predictive models for healthcare and operational challenges using Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, and deep learning. My work includes service-delay prediction, disease detection, NLP, and computer vision.

Explainable AI & Research

I investigate how machine learning can support reliable, understandable decisions. At the University of East London, I contribute to healthcare AI research, using SHAP, careful evaluation, and evidence-led analysis.

Data Analytics & Visualisation

I turn complex operational data into clear reports, dashboards, and KPIs. My experience at Brother Marcus includes analysing multi-site restaurant performance, service bottlenecks, and revenue trends to support management decisions.

Data Engineering & Automation

I develop data workflows and automated reporting tools using Python, SQL, Flask, and APIs. I also bring experience in AI dataset preparation, annotation quality control, and consistent data inputs for model training.

Software & Web Development

I build API-driven applications and practical web tools. My projects include travel-booking workflows with Flask, Redis, and Sabre APIs, and EveCode, a learning platform with an integrated compiler and file management.

Teaching & Technical Collaboration

I support learning through tutoring, technical guidance, and collaborative research. My experience includes teaching assistance at ULAB, guiding AI data trainers, and coordinating development work across project teams.
