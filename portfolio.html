An Explainable AI Decision-Support Framework for Service Delay Prediction in Multi-Restaurant Operations

1 / 1

Jan 2026 – Mar 2026

Predicts four restaurant service-delay outcomes using operational data, machine learning, SHAP explanations, and a recommendation layer.

Developed an explainable AI decision-support framework to predict and interpret operational delays across multi-restaurant operations. The project used transaction-level POS data from eight Brother Marcus restaurant locations, covering January 2025 to March 2026, including order, item, modifier, payment, and service-level information.

The system predicted four key operational outcomes: food service delay, drinks service delay, order completion delay, and table turnover delay. Multiple machine learning models were evaluated, including Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Gaussian Naïve Bayes, and XGBoost, with XGBoost producing the strongest overall performance.

UniTrack: A RFID Attendance System

Oct 2022 – Nov 2022

An RFID attendance system using Bootstrap, PHP, and MySQL to manage university courses, devices, teachers, and students.

Overview UniTrack is a comprehensive attendance management system designed specifically for universities. It has been developed using Bootstrap, PHP, MySQL, and RFID modules to provide a user-friendly interface and efficient administration. The system enables universities to seamlessly manage semesters, departments, RFID devices, courses, programs, teachers, and students. With UniTrack, administrators can easily create classes and assign teachers and students to those classes. Teachers, upon receiving their account credentials, can effortlessly add students to the classes assigned to them by the admin. The attendance tracking process is simplified as teachers can activate the RFID device in their class, and as students swipe their cards, attendance is automatically recorded. UniTrack simplifies attendance management for universities, providing accurate and convenient tracking while reducing administrative burdens.
